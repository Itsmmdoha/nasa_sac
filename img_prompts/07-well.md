# Well

Save as: `public/field/well.png`
Size: 1000 × 1000, transparent background

The brick mouth of the well only. The bucket is a separate picture and has to be able to drop into this opening, so leave the mouth open and do not paint a rope or a bucket.

## Prompt

A small round village well, flat gouache, seen from a slight side angle, isolated on transparent. A low ring of clay bricks `#8C4E2A` and `#6B4A32`. Inside, dark well water `#0F4C5C` with one small pale reflection `#7EB6C4`. A short wooden post rises behind the ring, with no rope on it. The opening is clearly empty. The base of the ring sits near the bottom of the canvas. Soft edges, no outline, no text.

## Avoid

A bucket, a rope, a person, a pulley wheel with rope, flowers, a roof, text, a background landscape.
