# Bucket

Save as: `public/field/bucket.png`
Size: 600 × 900, transparent background

Hung from the well post and animated downward into the water. Draw it with a short rope stub at the top so it has something to hang from. The rope should start at the top center of the canvas.

## Prompt

A small wooden well bucket, flat gouache, isolated on transparent. Wood `#6B4A32`, a simple tapered cup, open at the top. A short rope rises from the handle to the top center of the canvas. No water pouring, no splash. Soft edges, no outline, no text. Centered, with the rope touching the top edge and the bucket in the upper half so there is room below it when it drops.

## Avoid

A person, a well, a metal pail with a logo, a stream of water, text, a background.
