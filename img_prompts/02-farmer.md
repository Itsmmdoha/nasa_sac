# Farmer

Save as: `public/field/farmer.png`
Size: 800 × 1200, transparent background

One standing figure. The feet touch the bottom edge so the picture can pivot from the ground. Leave empty space above the head. Seen from behind and a little to the side, looking toward the right, where the crops are.

## Prompt

A single Barind farmer painted in flat gouache, full body, standing still, weight on both bare feet. Viewed from behind, three-quarter, face not really visible. Faded indigo shirt `#3E5C8A`. Off-white lungi `#F4EFE4` to the ankles, with a narrow navy waist stripe `#1F4E79`. A red gamcha `#B4332A` wrapped on the head, one tail hanging down the right shoulder. Skin `#C68642`. The right arm hangs a little away from the body so it can be posed later; the left arm is quiet. No jewelry, no phone, no tool. Soft painted edges, no black outline, no cartoon eyes. Isolated on a transparent background. Feet flat on the bottom of the canvas, centered.

## Avoid

Front-facing portrait, smile, big eyes, hat that is not a gamcha, boots, jeans, text, shadow blob bigger than the feet, a second person, a background scene, green screen spill on the shirt.
