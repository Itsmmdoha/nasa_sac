# Mustard

Save as: `public/field/mustard.png`
Size: 800 × 1200, transparent background

One mustard plant, roots or stem base at the bottom center. It will sway from that point and also appear small in the zone count, so the shape has to read at a glance.

## Prompt

One rabi mustard plant, flat gouache, isolated on transparent. A slim green stem and a loose head of round yellow flowers, `#E6B325` and `#F2D04B`, a few deeper gold buds. About four blossoms, not a whole field. The plant is taller than it is wide. Stem meets the bottom center of the canvas. Soft painted edges, no outline, no pot, no soil mound, no label.

## Avoid

A field of mustard, a person, text, a vase, leaves that look like a tree, photoreal petals, black contour lines.
