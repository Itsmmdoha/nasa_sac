# Lentil

Save as: `public/field/lentil.png`
Size: 800 × 1200, transparent background

Shorter than the mustard and the maize. Same bottom-center anchor. This plant grows larger when it is the chosen crop, so keep the shape simple and centered.

## Prompt

One lentil plant, flat gouache, isolated on transparent. A low bush: two or three short stems and small oval leaves in `#2F6B3A` and `#3D8B4F`, with two tiny pale pods. Clearly shorter than a maize stalk. The base sits on the bottom center of the canvas, with empty space above. Soft edges, no outline, no soil, no pot, no text.

## Avoid

A climbing vine, yellow flowers, a maize cob, a field, a person, a label, black contour lines.
