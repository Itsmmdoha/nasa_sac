# Plot backdrop

Save as: `public/field/plot-backdrop.png`
Size: 1920 × 800

This is the header background. The farmer, crops, and well are drawn on top of it later, so the middle of the ground must stay empty.

## Prompt

A wide gouache painting of an empty dry-season field in the Barind tract, Rajshahi, Bangladesh, late afternoon. The top half is a hazy sky, `#C5DCEA` at the top melting into warm dust `#F6E2B8` near the horizon. A soft sun `#F6C453` sits in the upper right, not a starburst, just a flat disc with a faint warm halo. The horizon is low and flat, about two thirds of the way down the picture. Below it, cracked red clay `#C9844A` with a paler crust `#E3B888` and a few dark hairline cracks `#8C4E2A`. The center of the field, where people and crops will stand, is quiet: no plants, no stones big enough to hide a figure, no path leading out of frame. Far on the horizon, two or three tiny dark trees and a hint of a homestead, small enough to ignore. The ground rises slightly in the middle so figures have a ridge to stand on. Painterly, flat shapes, no camera lens, no text.

## Avoid

Farmer, person, animal, crop, flower, maize, mustard, well, bucket, tractor, drone, road sign, text, watermark, lush green grass, rice paddy, mountains, clouds that look like clipart, a frame or border.
