# Maize

Save as: `public/field/maize.png`
Size: 800 × 1200, transparent background

The tall crop. It droops when the farmer is trying to save water, so the stalk should be one clear vertical shape with the cob readable from a distance.

## Prompt

One maize plant, flat gouache, isolated on transparent. A straight green stalk nearly the full height of the canvas, two broad leaves in `#3E6B32`, and one yellow cob `#F2C14E` with a few husk lines. Base of the stalk on the bottom center. Soft painted edges, no outline, no soil, no pot, no text. It should not look like a cactus.

## Avoid

A cactus, a corn maze, many cobs, popcorn, a person, a field, black contour lines, a label.
