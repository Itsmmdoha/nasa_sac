# Seedling

Save as: `public/field/seedling.png`
Size: 800 × 1200, transparent background

Used when the assigned rabi crop is not mustard, lentil, or maize. A young unspecified crop, still clearly a plant.

## Prompt

One young crop seedling, flat gouache, isolated on transparent. A short green stem and two simple leaves, `#3E6B32` and `#67A85A`. No flower, no cob, no grain head. Base on the bottom center of the canvas, lots of empty space above. Soft edges, no outline, no soil, no pot, no text.

## Avoid

Mustard flowers, a maize cob, a lentil bush, a tree, a person, a label, black contour lines.
